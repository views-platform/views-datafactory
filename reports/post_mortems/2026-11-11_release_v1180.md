## What the defect was

UCDP event 463131 carries `best = 113,368` with `date_prec = 5` and `where_prec = 5` — UCDP stating
it knows neither the month nor the location beyond `where_description = "Tigray, Afar, Amhara"`,
across `date_start = 2021-01-01` to `date_end = 2021-12-31`. The pipeline read neither precision
code. It booked the entire total onto the closing month and the regional centroid cell, delivering
**113,395** state-based fatalities into one 0.5° cell-month whose own median is about 2, ringed by
zeros.

Downstream, that single cell-month became roughly **63%** of views-hydranet's primary evaluation
score. Month 504's state-based slice had an effective sample size of **1.04** — one number wearing
the costume of a distribution.

---

## What shipped

`refresh_pipeline.sh` now builds *and compiles* under `kgi_kti_activity`. Both axes are allocated
across the span and region UCDP actually declares, weighted by observed activity in the same
conflict, conserving `best`, `low` and `high` exactly by largest-remainder apportionment.

### The switch adds deaths; it does not only move them

The retired `production_parity` profile carried `exclude_where_prec = (4, 6)` — it *dropped* UCDP
events located only to an admin-1 region or to a country. Removing that filter recovers **43,933
events carrying 785,626 fatalities (+22.6%** on total `best`), of which 36,608 are state-based. Row
count goes **372,303 → 2,654,111 (7.1×)**.

Note what the filter did *not* do: it dropped `where_prec` 4 and 6 and **kept 5** — the class that
produced the Tigray artifact. It was never a precision threshold, and ADR-049 documented the filter
and said it "loses real deaths" without ever stating how many. The counter that would have answered,
`n_excluded_where_prec`, had existed since 2026-06-28 and had never been read back.

### The three observers

ADR-053 mandated three and nothing implemented any of them — no test in the repository referenced
ADR-053, `kgi_kti_activity`, or the seasonality guard at all. Turning a decision on without its
declared observers is C-365 recurring, which is why these were in scope rather than deferred:

1. **Composed golden criterion** — event 463131 conserves exactly through both stages; the artifact
   cell-month falls below 1,000; the event occupies at least 10 months and 10 cells.
2. **December seasonality guard** — December's share of state-based fatalities below 9.0%. ADR-053's
   own pre-registered falsifier. Demonstrated red at 12.27% against the old profile.
3. **Provenance counters** in the viewpoint ledger — `n_temporally_distributed`,
   `temporal_weight_key_counts`, `n_temporal_uniform_fallback`.

## Incident: a fail-green trap, found in planning

`build_viewpoint.py` writes to `data/viewpoint/<profile>.parquet` when given no `--output`.
`compile_grid.py` defaults `--source` to `data/viewpoint/production_parity.parquet`. The pipeline
passed neither.

So changing only step 3's `--profile` token would have had step 3 write the new parquet and step 4
recompile **the old one**. The UCDP digest would not move, ADR-041's content-addressed skip would
correctly skip assembly and export, and the run would have reported **success having changed
nothing** — after a 7.5-hour run, with the defect fully intact and a green log saying otherwise.

The fix is one variable used twice: `VIEWPOINT_PROFILE` near the top, consumed by step 3's
`--profile` and by `VIEWPOINT_PARQUET` for step 4's `--source`. A variable cannot drift from itself.
The replacement guard asserts step 4 derives its source from the same variable, so it catches the
trap rather than the token; the mutation drill pointed step 4 back at the parity filename and the
test went red.

This was caught by reading the two scripts' defaults during planning, not by any test. **No
mechanism existed that would have caught it**, and that is the finding — the previous guard asserted
a literal string and would have passed a wiring error happily.

## Incident: a register claim that named the wrong tripwire

C-356, C-364 and C-366 each stated that views-postprocessing pinned a digest a profile change would
move. It does not. `test_gaul_lookup_fidelity.py` pins a lookup-version *format* against synthetic
digests and checks GAUL cell→country assignment; a UCDP allocation change touches no GAUL parquet,
so it would never have fired. Corrected in three places plus the changelog.

The real hard tripwire is views-hydranet's exact-value gate. It is **not cited by path here**, per
C-358: naming a sibling repository's file as the thing that breaks reads as attribution even when
the repository is public. Visibility is the floor, not the test.

## Honest negatives

Recorded because a post-mortem that reports only confirmations is a highlight reel.

**Peaks do not fall monotonically.** Event 463131's *own* peak cell-month **rises**, 3,471 under
uniform allocation to 11,732 under activity weighting, because mass moves toward months with
observed fighting. That is the mechanism working. The claim is narrower than "peaks fall": the
*artifact* cell-month collapses and the slice stops being one number.

**Seasonality improves but does not flatten.** December's share falls 12.27% → 6.62%, under the 9.0%
guard. But **August rises 6.66% → 11.30%** and becomes the largest month, mostly event 463137
(121,848 deaths, Aug–Oct 2022) relocating toward precisely-dated fighting. Not a new artifact, but
not "seasonality is fixed" either.

**A build-time discrepancy was not isolated.** The local build took 136 s against ADR-053's recorded
425.6 s. Page-cache warmth is a hypothesis, not a finding, and it is recorded as unexplained rather
than explained away.

### views-frames 1.10.2 → 3.0.0 on the production box

`uv sync` moved the server across two major versions. This is not a v1.18.0 change: the server had
skipped v1.17.0 (tagged 2026-11-02, deliberately not deployed), and v1.17.0 is where the cap widened
to `<4`. v1.17.0's window measured that crossing in a clean venv — full suite, `mypy --strict`, ruff,
**including ADR-050's fixture drift alarm**, which is what establishes that 3.0.0 writes a
byte-identical FeatureFrame layout. The clean-venv install of 1.18.0 also resolved views-frames
3.0.0, so the server matches what CI and PyPI tested. **Not a confound for the numbers below.**

## Release topology

`development` → `main` through PR **#544**, merged as a **merge commit** `4052498` (two parents read
back). Auto-merge armed with `scripts/arm_automerge.sh 544 merge`, which performs the GraphQL
disable/enable pair and **reads the method back** — `gh pr merge --auto` silently refuses to change
the method on an already-armed PR, and a squash onto `main` rewrites the release SHAs and breaks the
ancestry the back-merge maintains (C-340).

Tag `v1.18.0` → `4052498`, lightweight to match every prior tag. Release published from `main`;
publishing fires on `release: published`, not on a tag push. Back-merge via PR **#546**, merge
commit `703fbbb`.

The **Release hygiene** workflow failed its `topology` job at 20:44 UTC and opened tracking issue
#545. That is correct behaviour, not an incident: between promotion and back-merge the branches
genuinely are diverged. It was dispatched again after #546 landed, went green, and closed #545
itself.

## What to do differently

**TBM** — numbered, after the run. Candidates already earned in this window:

1. **Read the release and deploy guides yourself before relying on them.** A subagent read them and
   reported; reading them directly then found two defects the report missed — `server_quickref.md`'s
   expected-totals table (which would have read a healthy run as broken) and
   `publishing_to_pypi.md`'s break-glass JSON (which, restored verbatim, would have *weakened*
   branch protection as the final step of a procedure whose purpose is to restore it).
2. **A local check reported green is not the suite reported green.** Three register guards were run
   by hand and reported as passing; the full suite then failed on a fourth, a 704-character header
   line against a 700-character budget. Report the scope of what was actually run.
3. **Lint the whole tree, not CI's subset.** CI lints `src/ tests/ scripts/` only, so sixteen
   findings sat in a notebook with CI green. `ruff check .` locally is the honest command.
4. **Switching a profile means auditing every consumer of the old artifact path.** The status
   generator and two test fixtures hardcoded `production_parity.parquet`; the fixtures would have
   **skipped**, and a conservation check that skips looks exactly like one that passes.
